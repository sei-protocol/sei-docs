This guide helps you set up your wallet and start using Sei, even if you are new to blockchain.

## 1. Get a wallet

Download a compatible wallet, such as [MetaMask](https://metamask.io/), [Rabby](https://rabby.io/), [Binance Wallet](https://www.binance.com/en/web3wallet), or any other [supported wallet](/learn/wallets).

## 2. Set up and "associate" your wallet

Any transaction that you make broadcasts your public key to the chain. This gives your wallet full cross-environment functionality. If your wallet is not "associated", the network does not know that your key exists. A cryptographic algorithm derives the EVM wallet address from the key. Without the key, the network cannot tell which two addresses belong to the same wallet.

<Info>
  Learn more about [how accounts work](/learn/accounts) on Sei.
</Info>

## 3. Get tokens

- [Bridge from another network](https://welcome.symph.ag)
- [Transfer from another account](https://dashboard.sei.io/transfer)
- [Use the faucet](/learn/faucet) (Sei Testnet only)
- Transfer from a centralized exchange (CEX) to your wallet address

<Warning>
  Check your CEX's guidelines to make sure that your transfers go smoothly. Withdrawals from a CEX typically do not need a memo, but deposits back to a CEX may need one.
</Warning>

## 4. Explore the ecosystem

Sei has a diverse ecosystem of dApps, including DeFi and games. To find the latest projects, explore the [Sei Ecosystem Hub](https://www.sei.io/ecosystem) or follow [@SeiNetwork](https://x.com/SeiNetwork) on X.
