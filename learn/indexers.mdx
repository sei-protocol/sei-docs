Indexers collect and organize blockchain data so that dApps can query Sei data at scale. They have structured APIs and real-time updates, and they perform significantly better than direct queries to nodes.

## Why use indexers?

- Query millions of transactions in milliseconds with optimized database structures.
- Filter, sort, and aggregate blockchain data with GraphQL and REST APIs.
- Receive live blockchain data through WebSocket connections and webhooks.
