## Introduction

The Sei network supports various crypto assets, including:

- Native tokens ($SEI)
- ERC20 tokens
- ERC721 NFTs

A Ledger hardware wallet manages your keys securely when you interact with the
Sei network.

## Requirements

Before you start, make sure that you have:

- A Ledger wallet device
- The [Ledger Live](https://www.ledger.com/ledger-live) app, installed on your
  desktop or mobile device
- A compatible wallet application for Sei (see the Sei documentation for
  recommended wallets)
- A USB cable to connect your device (some models can also use Bluetooth)
- The latest firmware on your Ledger device
- An active internet connection

## Installation instructions

### Install Ledger Live

1. **Download Ledger Live**: Go to the
   [official Ledger website](https://www.ledger.com/start). Download Ledger
   Live for your operating system.

2. **Install Ledger Live**: Follow the installation instructions for your
   platform.

3. **Set up Ledger Live**: Open Ledger Live. Then follow the on-screen
   instructions to set up your Ledger device.

4. **Install the Sei app on Ledger**:
   - In Ledger Live, go to **My Ledger**.
   - Connect and unlock your Ledger device.
   - Search for "Sei" in the App Catalog. Then click **Install**.

<Info>On Linux, add a `udev rule` to allow access to the device. You can find the script to add a rule in the [Ledger udev-rules repository](https://github.com/LedgerHQ/udev-rules).</Info>

## Setup instructions

### Using a compatible wallet application

1. **Connect your Ledger device**:

   - Open your compatible wallet application for Sei.
   - Connect your Ledger device to your computer.
   - Unlock your Ledger device and open the Sei app.

2. **Access the Sei network**:

   - In your wallet application, select the option to connect with a Ledger
     device.
   - Follow the prompts to connect to your Ledger device.

## Viewing account balance

1. Open your wallet application for Sei.
2. Make sure that your Ledger device is connected and that the Sei app is open.
3. Your account balance and asset details should appear on the main screen of
   the wallet application.

## How to receive crypto assets

1. **Navigate to the Receive section**:

   - In your wallet application, go to the **Receive** section or a similar
     section.
   - If applicable, select the crypto asset that you want to receive.

2. **Verify the receiver address**:

   - The wallet application displays the receiver address.
   - Verify that the address on the screen matches the address on your Ledger
     device.
   - To confirm the address, press the appropriate buttons on your Ledger
     device.

<Warning>Always verify the receiver address on your Ledger device before you share it. This prevents attacks such as address spoofing.</Warning>

3. **Share the address**:
   - Copy the verified address. Share it with the sender, or paste it into an
     exchange withdrawal form.

## How to send crypto assets

1. **Navigate to the Send section**:

   - Go to the **Send** section (or equivalent) in your wallet application.
   - Select the crypto asset that you want to send.

2. **Enter transaction details**:

   - Enter the recipient's address.
   - Enter the amount that you want to send.
   - (Optional) Add a memo if one is required.

3. **Submit the transaction**:

   - Start the send process in your wallet application.

4. **Verify and confirm on the Ledger device**:
   - Review the transaction details on the Ledger device.
   - Verify that the recipient's address, amount, and other transaction details
     are correct.
   - To confirm and broadcast the transaction, press the appropriate buttons
     on your Ledger device.

<Warning>Always double-check the transaction details on your Ledger device before you confirm. This check is your last protection against possible attacks or errors.</Warning>
