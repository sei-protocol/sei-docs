## Usage notes

- **Rate limits**: Each address can request tokens once per day. Wait 24 hours between requests.
- **Testnet only**: Tokens are distributed on Sei Testnet and cannot be used on Sei Mainnet.
- **Gas coverage**: One request gives enough SEI to cover hundreds of transactions on Sei Testnet.

## Testnet USDC

If you need USDC to test ERC-20 workflows, use the Circle Faucet to get USDC on Sei Testnet.

- [Circle Faucet](https://faucet.circle.com)
