**Address:** `0x0000000000000000000000000000000000001008`

<Danger>**Retired as of v6.4.0:** Migrate to a third-party oracle provider, such as [Chainlink](/evm/oracles/chainlink), [Pyth](/evm/oracles/pyth-network), [RedStone](/evm/oracles/redstone), or [API3](/evm/oracles/api3). The native Sei Oracle precompile is retired, and on-chain oracle data queries are disabled. A call to `getExchangeRates` or `getOracleTwaps` reverts with the error `oracle precompile is retired; oracle data queries are disabled`.</Danger>
