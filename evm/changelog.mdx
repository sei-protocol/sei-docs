**v6.8 chain upgrade**

The v6.8 chain upgrade introduces new versioned EVM precompiles that expose additional on-chain query and authorization methods to contract developers. Node operators must upgrade to the v6.8 binary before the scheduled plan height; an unupgraded binary halts at that height.

Newly exposed precompile methods include:

- **Auth** (`0x…100D`): `account`, `accounts`, `params`, `nextAccountNumber`.
- **Authz** (`0x…100E`): `grants`, `granterGrants`, `granteeGrants`.
- **Evidence** (`0x…100F`): `evidence`, `allEvidence`.
- **Bank** (`0x…1001`): adds `spendableBalances`, `totalSupply`, `params`, `denomMetadata`, `denomsMetadata` alongside the existing send/balance methods.
- **Distribution** (`0x…1007`): adds `communityPool`, `params`, `validatorOutstandingRewards`, `validatorCommission`, `validatorSlashes`, `delegationRewards`, `delegatorValidators`, `delegatorWithdrawAddress`, `grantWithdrawAuthorization`, `revokeWithdrawAuthorization`, `withdrawDelegationRewardsWithAuthorization`, `withdrawValidatorCommissionWithAuthorization`.
- **Gov** (`0x…1006`): adds `proposal`, `proposals`, `getVote`, `votes`, `params`, `getDeposit`, `deposits`, `tallyResult`, `grantVoteAuthorization`, `grantProposalAuthorization`, `voteWithAuthorization`, `submitProposalWithAuthorization`, `revokeVoteAuthorization`, `revokeProposalAuthorization`, `voteWeighted`.

</Note>
