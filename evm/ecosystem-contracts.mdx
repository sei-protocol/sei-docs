### How to use this registry

- To find the contracts of a specific project quickly, search by project name in the search bar.
- To see detailed information about a contract, click its Seiscan link.
- Bookmark this page for quick access to the latest contract addresses and updates.

<Tip>

**Add your project**

To add your project or its contracts to this registry, contact us in the [Sei Tech Chat](https://t.me/+KZdhZ1eE-G01NmZk). We will help you get listed.

</Tip>
